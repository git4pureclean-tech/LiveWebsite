Who comes to your home

A uniformed technician arrives in a marked van. We wear shoe covers, protect furniture and keep you informed from arrival through drying.

Equipment and products

We use truck-mounted extraction for carpet and plant-based cleaning solutions. Homes, condos and offices across North York and the GTA can request a visit.

Insurance and our guarantee

PureClean carries $2 million in liability coverage and has WSIB coverage. If a result needs more attention, we offer a free return clean, with a refund if you remain dissatisfied afterward.
