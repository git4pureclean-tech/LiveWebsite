Website analytics and privacy

If you allow analytics, PureClean counts website page views and interactions such as phone link taps, WhatsApp taps and video plays. Each count includes the page, control, date and screen size category. The report uses aggregate counts and does not store a visitor identifier, IP address, contact details or anything entered in the contact form.

Contact details entered in the form go into the message you review in WhatsApp. You decide whether to send it there.

Analytics is off until you accept it. We store your choice in this browser so you can change it at any time here. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control, analytics stays off.
