Website analytics and privacy

PureClean counts website page views and interactions such as phone link taps, WhatsApp opens and video plays. Each count includes the page, control, date and screen size category. The report uses aggregate counts and does not store a visitor identifier, IP address, contact details or anything entered in the contact form.

Contact details entered in the form go into the message you review in WhatsApp. You decide whether to send it there.

Your browser’s Global Privacy Control signal turns off these counts. You can also opt out on this device.
