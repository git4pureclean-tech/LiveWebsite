What your visit includes

The technician pre-treats the carpet and works on individual spots before extracting the soil with hot water and suction. Standard spot treatment is included with residential cleaning.

Hot water extraction is often called steam cleaning. The equipment rinses the carpet and lifts the cleaning solution and loosened soil back out. Let us know which rooms, stairs and landings are carpeted so the quote reflects the whole job.

Getting the room back in use

For most carpets, plan on about two hours before light foot traffic and four to six hours for drying. Ask your technician about your rooms before moving furniture back.

Set aside toys and small items before the visit. If heavy furniture or a building rule affects access to an area, tell us when you book. If you’re deciding between renting a machine and booking, see our DIY versus professional carpet cleaning guide.

If an odour keeps coming back

A spill that has reached the underpad can need a separate treatment. Describe any lingering odour at booking so we can explain the options.

Questions about this service

Can you clean carpet in a condo?

Yes. Tell us about the unit, parking and building access when you call so the team can plan the visit.

How can I prepare for the visit?

Clear small items from the carpet and mention any heavy furniture, stairs or building access restrictions when you book.

What affects a carpet cleaning quote?

Tell us which rooms, stairs and landings need cleaning and flag any persistent odours or stains. For condos, include parking and access details.
