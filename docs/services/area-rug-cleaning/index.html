Cleaning at home

Most area rugs can be cleaned on site. Show the technician the care label, fringes, stains and any earlier spot treatments when they arrive.

Let us know where the rug sits, how much foot traffic it gets and whether a spill reached the backing or the floor below it. If the rug has a care label, send a photo of it along with a picture of the full rug.

Larger and delicate pieces

Pickup and delivery can be arranged for select rugs. Ask when booking so we can discuss the rug, access and how the service will work for your piece.

Getting an estimate

Let us know the dimensions and where the rug sits. A photo of the whole rug and a close-up of a problem area can help you explain what you need.

Measure the length and width and point out fringe, stains or any damaged edges. If you also need the carpet underneath cleaned, ask about residential carpet cleaning when requesting the estimate.

Questions about this service

Is pickup required for every rug?

Most rugs can be cleaned where they are. The team can discuss pickup if size or material calls for it.

What should I send for a rug cleaning estimate?

Send the rug’s measurements, a photo of the whole rug, close-ups of problem spots and a picture of the care label if there is one.

Can I get a quote for a wool or hand-knotted rug?

Yes. Share the rug’s material if you know it. PureClean can discuss the fibre, dye stability and whether on-site cleaning or pickup suits the rug.
