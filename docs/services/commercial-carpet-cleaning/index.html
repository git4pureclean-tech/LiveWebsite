Offices, stores and shared buildings

We clean office suites, retail carpet and condo common areas. Tell us which spaces see the heaviest use and when your tenants, customers or staff need access.

Busy entrances and paths between desks can wear differently from meeting rooms or closed offices. Share the flooring layout and the areas you want cleaned so the quote can account for the spaces in use.

Plan around opening hours

Ask about an after-hours visit. Share building access instructions and the approximate floor area so the team can confirm the scope, timing and price before arrival.

When asking for a quote, share the approximate carpeted square footage, the operating hours, the earliest time the space can be accessed and when it needs to reopen. Let us know about access cards, parking or loading areas as well.

Extraction and follow-up

Truck-mounted extraction is available on commercial jobs. If an area needs another pass after the visit, contact the team about the free re-clean guarantee.

See photos and footage of office carpet and extraction projects to get a sense of the equipment and work involved.

Questions about this service

Can property managers request a quote?

Yes. Include the building location, shared areas to clean, and any entry or scheduling restrictions.

Can you clean after the office closes?

Ask about after-hours availability and tell us when staff leave and when the space needs to reopen.

What should I include with a commercial quote request?

Share an approximate carpeted area, the property type, your preferred time window and photos of the busiest or most heavily marked areas.
