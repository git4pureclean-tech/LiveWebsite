What we clean

Book a sofa, sectional or upholstered dining chairs. Include the number of pieces, upholstery material if known, and any visible marks in your quote request.

For a sectional, tell us how many pieces and loose cushions you want cleaned. For dining chairs, say whether the seats, backs or both are upholstered. Photos help identify fabric and areas needing attention.

Treatment for the fabric

We use plant-based products and tailor the treatment to the material. Point out care instructions and previous spot treatments before cleaning starts.

If there is a manufacturer’s care tag, send a photo of it before booking. Mention any previous spot cleaners or protectants used on the fabric so the technician knows what to check.

Optional fabric protection

Ask about a protective finish after cleaning, particularly for dining chairs or a sofa that gets daily use. The treatment helps buy time to blot future spills.

If you are booking for a furnished room, ask about combining the visit with carpet cleaning or area rug cleaning.

Questions about this service

Can I combine upholstery and carpet cleaning?

Yes. Choose multiple services when requesting a quote and list the pieces and rooms you would like cleaned.

Can you clean sectional sofas and dining chairs?

Yes. Include the number of sectional pieces or chairs, a photo of the furniture and any available care tag when you ask for a quote.

Do you need to know which spot cleaner I used?

Yes. Share the product name if you have it and describe where you applied it. Mention any changes you noticed after the fabric dried.
